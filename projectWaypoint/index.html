✦ Backup ✦

Every character on this device in one file — sheets, portraits and journals. The app keeps them in this browser’s storage and nowhere else, so a cleared browser is a lost roster unless you have one of these.

✦ Compendium ✦

The game’s content — classes, subclasses, traits, skills and gear. Characters store ids and read every number out of here, so replacing this re-points every sheet at once.

✦ Appearance ✦

Create Character

✦ Choose a Class ✦

Nine classes across three families. Choosing one grants its Tier 0 Trait. Tap a name to read it before you commit.

✦ Choose a Subclass ✦

Your subclass grants a second Tier 0 Trait, and sets your health, wounds, mana and what you are trained to carry.

✦ Assign Attributes ✦

Every attribute starts at 0. You have four points to place, one point to an attribute at a time.

✦ Assign Skills ✦

Four points, each worth +2, into four different skills. Later levels may stack onto these or open new ones.

✦ Choose Your Loadout ✦

Two hands’ worth of weapons and shields, armour your class is trained in, and three pieces of adventuring gear.

✦ Define Your Origin ✦

A character needs a name.

Backstory

Entirely up to you, and entirely optional — show up and discover your character at the table if you would rather. If you do want prompts: who are you, where did you grow up, how did you end up here, what did you do before this, are you law-abiding, do locals know your name?
