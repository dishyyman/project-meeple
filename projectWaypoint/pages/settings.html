Settings

✦ Backup ✦

Every character on this device in one file — sheets, portraits and journals. The app keeps them in this browser’s storage and nowhere else, so a cleared browser is a lost roster unless you have one of these.

✦ Compendium ✦

The game’s content — classes, subclasses, traits, skills and gear. Characters store ids and read every number out of here, so replacing this re-points every sheet at once.

✦ Appearance ✦

Not wired up yet. These remember what you pick, but the app keeps its parchment either way until the theme work lands.
